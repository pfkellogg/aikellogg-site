Install it on your FM-1

  1. Plug in your FM-1 by USB. It needs SLOOP 2.6 or later (the Big Screen builds of SLOOP work too).
  2. Stop the song on the FM-1 (press STOP).
  3. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, press Install and allow MIDI.
  4. On Windows, close other apps that use the FM-1 first (Windows lets only one app use it at a time).

It goes into an empty sample slot (USR1 to USR4) and an empty sound slot (U01 to U32). Nothing on your FM-1 is replaced unless you choose it. Installing again updates it in place.



Then on the FM-1: turn PRESETS past the factory sounds to AIK GRAND, or SAVE › USER, pick its slot, LOAD.

What it is

Eight notes of a Yamaha C5 concert grand, from G2 to F5, each covering about five keys. They fit into one of SLOOP's own sample slots (80 KB). Each note rings on with a seamless loop, and the FM-1's envelope lets it fade like a real piano. It is a small piano for a small synth: next to the full recordings on a computer it is simpler, but for a sound that lives inside the FM-1 it is about as good as the hardware allows.

Files, for tinkerers: aik-grand.hdraik-grand.binaik-grand-sound.jsoninstaller.js (SLOOP's editor protocol: the sample slot header and data, and the sound's 71 values).
